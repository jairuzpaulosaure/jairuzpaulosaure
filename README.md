# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an Assistant System Administrator and LMS Support Specialist with nearly seven years of progressive IT experience, beginning as a Computer Laboratory Custodian during high school, continuing as a Technical Support Student Assistant while pursuing my bachelor's degree, and advancing through multiple promotions into my current role.

Throughout my career, I have supported enterprise IT operations by administering Active Directory, managing Windows environments, supporting Learning Management Systems (Moodle), troubleshooting technical issues, and assisting with system administration and infrastructure maintenance. Working in both academic and enterprise environments has strengthened my technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

I recently completed my **Master of Information Technology (MIT)** and am continuously expanding my expertise in Windows Server, Linux, AWS Cloud, networking, automation, and infrastructure engineering. My long-term goal is to become a **Cloud Infrastructure Engineer**, building reliable, secure, and scalable systems while continuously learning modern cloud technologies.

Outside of my primary role, I also enjoy teaching programming and mentoring aspiring developers, helping bridge the gap between technical concepts and practical application.
